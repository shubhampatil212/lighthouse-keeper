# Lighthouse Keeper — release notes

## 1.0 — November 2026

The first release. Lighthouse Keeper is a calm, tense little game about light. It's night, the fog is in, and the ships can't see the reefs. Swing your lighthouse beam to guide them home.

### What's in it

- **A campaign of 30 nights** across five regions: Harbor Bay, Northern Ice, Storm Strait, Smugglers’ Cove and the Archipelago. Each night ends in a dawn, with up to three stars and a bonus goal.
- **Six kinds of ship.** Fishing boats, slow-turning cargo ships, sailboats the wind pushes about, precious ferries that must not be lost, tugboats that help, and smugglers you catch by keeping them in the light.
- **The sea fights back.** Fog bands that swallow your beam, drifting icebergs, lightning, storm waves, whirlpools and pirate ships hunting in the dark.
- **Your tools.** The foghorn, flares, a second lighthouse on some nights, and bell buoys you place yourself.
- **The harbor shop.** Spend the coins your ships bring home on a wider lens, a brighter lamp, faster gears, a quicker foghorn and more flares. The island grows a cottage, a dock, a chapel, a market and festival lights as you go.
- **Endless watch and the daily sea.** Endless watch opens after Harbor Bay. The daily sea is the same sea for every keeper, each day.
- **The keeper's logbook.** Diary pages, and an entry for every ship and danger you've met.
- **Sound made live in your browser.** Music that grows tense when a ship is in danger, and the sea, wind, rain and gulls of each region. No downloads.

### Play it your way

- **Phone or computer.** One-handed in portrait on a phone, mouse or keyboard on a computer.
- **Install it.** Add it to your home screen from the title screen (Android) or Safari's Share menu (iPhone). It then runs full screen and **works offline**.
- **Three difficulties:** Calm, Keeper and Storm. Change them any time.
- **Accessibility:**
  - reduced motion;
  - colorblind-friendly harbor colors with shapes;
  - high-contrast reefs;
  - larger text;
  - left-handed buttons;
  - screen-reader announcements;
  - vibration on or off.
- **Graphics:** Low, Medium, High or Auto, which picks for your device.
- **Your progress saves itself** on your device. Export it to a file and import it on another device from Settings.

### Good to know

- The game saves to your browser. Clearing the browser's site data erases it, so **export your save** if you want to keep it safe.
- After an update, a phone that has the game installed shows the old version once more, then the new one.
- If something ever goes wrong, the game shows a "Something went wrong" message with a Reload button. Your progress is saved first.
