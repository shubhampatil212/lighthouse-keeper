# Balance report

Generated by `npm run playtest` on 2026-11-13: 40 nights per level, difficulty and bot. Do not edit by hand.

## Summary

- **Careful play:** the smart bot meets its target (at least 1.8 stars on Keeper) on 30 of 30 levels and wins at least 100% of nights on every level.
- **Careless play:** the sweeper fails within the 20%–40% target on 10 of 18 region 3–5 levels. The rest are listed under Outliers; the finales are meant to be harder.
- **Economy:** a ★★★ region pays 0.80–1.59× the cost of maxing Brighter lamp (average 1.23, target about 1.3). Every upgrade (1600 coins) becomes affordable in region 4 with ★★★ runs.
- **24 outlier notes** below. Most say a level "may be easy" for the smart bot, which knows every rock; check them against human play-tests before tuning (docs/TODO.md).

## How to read this
- **smart**: lights whichever ship is closest to trouble, drives pirates off, catches smugglers, uses the horn and flares. Plays like a careful player who knows the layout.
- **nearest-reef**: always lights the ship closest to a rock.
- **sweeper**: swings the beam around and back without looking at the ships.
- Bots play with the upgrades a typical keeper owns on reaching each region (`tools/playtest/kits.ts`).
- Targets on Keeper: smart averages at least 1.8 stars (★★ on most levels); the sweeper fails 20%–40% of region 3–5 levels; no level is more than 0.6 stars harder than the one before it in its region (finales excepted).
